I'm Matt Chen, a freshman at Stanford studying mathematics. My interests live at the intersection of math, robotics, and AI.

I'm drawn to problems where clean mathematical thinking meets messy real-world systems — control theory, optimization, learned representations, and the question of how to build machines that actually work in unstructured environments.

Outside of coursework, I think a lot about startups and where AI is heading. I believe we're in the early innings of something transformative, and I want to be building, not just watching.

This blog is where I write about things I'm learning and thinking about. Expect posts on math, ML, robotics, and occasionally, whatever else I find interesting.
