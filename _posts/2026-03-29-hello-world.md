This is my blog. I'm Matt — a freshman at Stanford studying math, spending most of my free cycles thinking about robotics and AI.

I've wanted to start writing for a while. Not because I think I have everything figured out, but because writing is how I figure things out. There's something about forcing yourself to explain an idea clearly that exposes all the gaps in your understanding.

Here's what I expect to write about:

- **Math** — things I'm learning in my courses or on my own. Linear algebra, analysis, probability, optimization. I'll try to make these posts useful, not just notes-to-self.
- **AI/ML** — papers I'm reading, experiments I'm running, thoughts on where the field is going.
- **Robotics** — the parts where math meets the physical world. Control, planning, perception.
- **Building things** — I'm interested in startups and in the craft of making things that work. Expect some posts on engineering and product thinking.

If any of that sounds interesting, stick around. More soon.
